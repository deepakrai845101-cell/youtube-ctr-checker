    What is CTR on YouTube?

    YouTube CTR (click-through rate) is the percentage of impressions that turn into views. It measures how often people click your video after seeing your thumbnail and title.

    What is a good CTR for YouTube channels?

    A good CTR for YouTube depends on niche, audience quality, and traffic source, but many channels see healthy ranges between 2% and 10%. The best benchmark is your own trend over time.

    How does the YouTube CTR calculator work?

    This YouTube CTR calculator uses a simple formula: clicks ÷ impressions × 100. For example, 1,000 impressions and 80 clicks equals an 8% click-through rate.
