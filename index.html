    Frequently Asked Questions

    Quick answers about CTR benchmarks, scoring logic, and how to use this analyzer effectively.

    What does YouTube CTR measure?

    YouTube CTR (click-through rate) is the percentage of impressions that turn into views. It measures how often people click your video after seeing your thumbnail and title.

    What is a strong CTR benchmark?

    CTR varies by niche, traffic source, and audience quality. Many channels see typical ranges between 2% and 10%, but your most useful benchmark is your own trend over time.

    How is the CTR score calculated?

    The analyzer uses the standard formula: clicks ÷ impressions × 100. For example, 80 clicks from 1,000 impressions equals an 8% CTR.

    Is this tool really free?

    Yes. You can run unlimited checks at no cost, with no signup required.

    Does this tool store my data?

    No. All analysis runs in your browser, and the tool does not collect, store, or transmit your titles, thumbnails, or inputs.

    How can I improve my CTR?

    Use specific titles, stronger hooks, and high-contrast thumbnails. Compare your results over time to identify what consistently improves click-through rate.
